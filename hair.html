Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome for minor, quick services such as haircuts, trims, and basic waxing. However, appointments are highly recommended or required for major services like Hair Coloring, Balayage, Rebonding, Keratin Treatments, and Bridal Makeup.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash and GCash. We currently do not accept Maya or Bank Transfers.

How do I book an appointment?

Since this is a promotional website, you cannot book directly here. Please call us at 0929 849 9432 or message our Facebook Page to secure your slot.
